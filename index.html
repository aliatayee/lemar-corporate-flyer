Choose a structure

Which setup fits your business?

The right choice depends on your activity, ownership, visa needs and how you plan to operate. We'll assess your requirements and recommend the structure that fits.

Trade across the whole UAE market

A mainland licence lets you work directly with the local market and government entities, with room to expand your activities and visa capacity as you grow.

  • Trade anywhere in the UAE without a local distributor
  • Eligible to bid for government and semi-government work
  • Broad range of commercial and professional activities
  • Office and visa capacity that scales with headcount

A focused ecosystem for your sector

Free zones offer a streamlined setup inside a defined operating framework, with sector-focused communities and clear ownership rules.

  • Streamlined incorporation and licensing process
  • Sector-specific zones with built-in business communities
  • Flexible packages, from flexi-desk to full office
  • Well suited to international trade and service businesses

A holding structure for international interests

An offshore company is built for holding and international business purposes rather than day-to-day operations inside the UAE market.

  • Suited to holding assets, shares and intellectual property
  • Designed for international business, not local trade
  • No UAE operational office or residence visas attached
  • Often used alongside a mainland or free zone entity
