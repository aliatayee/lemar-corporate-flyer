Mainland, Free Zone and Offshore — side by side

01 — Mainland

Trade across the whole UAE

Work directly with the local market and government entities, with room to expand as you grow.

  • Trade anywhere in the UAE, no local distributor needed
  • Eligible for government and semi-government work
  • Office and visa capacity that scales with headcount
02 — Free Zone

A focused ecosystem for your sector

Streamlined setup inside a defined operating framework, with sector-focused communities.

  • Streamlined incorporation and licensing process
  • Flexible packages, from flexi-desk to full office
  • Well suited to international trade and services
03 — Offshore

A holding structure for international interests

Built for holding and international business, rather than day-to-day operations in the UAE.

  • Suited to holding assets, shares and IP
  • No UAE operational office or residence visas attached
  • Often used alongside a mainland or free zone entity

The right choice depends on your activity, ownership, visa needs and how you plan to operate. We'll assess your requirements and recommend the structure that fits.
